📊 Online Retail Sales Analysis

🎯 Project Objective

Analyze online retail transaction data to identify revenue trends, customer purchasing behavior, regional performance, and seasonal sales patterns to support data-driven business decisions.

💼 Business Problem

Business leaders needed insights into sales performance, customer behavior, geographic markets, and seasonal demand to identify growth opportunities and improve strategic planning.

📂 Dataset

Source: Tata Group Data Analytics Job Simulation (Forage)

Dataset: Online Retail Transactions

Size: 500K+ retail transaction records

Data Included: Invoice details, products, quantities, unit prices, customers, countries, and transaction dates.

🔍 Actions Performed

Analyzed 500K+ retail transactions using Power BI and Excel.

Examined revenue trends, customer purchasing patterns, regional performance, and seasonal demand.

Designed and developed four interactive Power BI dashboards to visualize key business metrics and communicate insights.

🛠️ Tools Used

Power BI

Microsoft Excel

📈 Key Insights

November generated the highest revenue (£1.51M).

The Netherlands was the top international market (£285K, excluding the UK).

The highest-value customer contributed £280K in revenue.

💡 Recommendations

Optimize inventory planning for seasonal demand.

Strengthen customer retention initiatives.

Prioritize high-performing international markets.

Support business decisions with interactive dashboards.
